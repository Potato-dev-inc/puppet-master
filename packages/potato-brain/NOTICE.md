## The Fish persona

The Fish persona pack (`persona/packs/fish/`) is a fan character. It is a distilled, shortened retelling of a fan-made character card. Checking that this character may be shipped is item 1 of the release checklist. It is not a build blocker.

## What is not shipped

No art, pet assets, meme-pack images or gifs are included. The desktop pet and the web page of the original fishd are not part of this package. There is no chat-app adapter in phase 1.
